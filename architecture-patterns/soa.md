# 🔗 Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA)

## Definition

SOA is an architecture where applications are built using **independent services**, each representing a business capability.

---

## Key Concept

- Services communicate via a **central Enterprise Service Bus (ESB)**

---

## Characteristics

- Service reuse across systems
- Centralized communication
- Standardized contracts

---

## Advantages

- Reusable services
- Easier enterprise integration
- Scalable at organizational level

---

## Disadvantages

- ESB becomes bottleneck
- Complex and expensive setup
- Slower development

---

## Use Cases

- Large enterprise systems
- Government systems
