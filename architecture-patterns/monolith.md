# 🧱 Monolithic Architecture

## Definition

A monolithic architecture is a **single unified application** where all components (UI, business logic, and data access) are tightly coupled and deployed as one unit.

---

## Structure

- UI Layer
- Business Logic Layer
- Data Access Layer  
All inside one codebase.

---

## Advantages

- Simple to develop
- Easy deployment
- Fast initial development
- Good for small teams and MVPs

---

## Disadvantages

- Hard to scale
- Tight coupling
- Difficult maintenance in large systems
- Full redeployment needed for small changes

---

## Use Cases

- Startups
- MVP applications
- Small internal tools
