# 💥 Big Bang Model

## Definition

The Big Bang Model is an **unstructured development approach** where coding starts without formal planning.

---

## Characteristics

- No clear requirements  
- No formal process  
- Development starts immediately  
- Highly unpredictable  

---

## Advantages

- Very simple  
- Fast start  
- Good for experiments  

---

## Disadvantages

- Extremely high risk  
- No scalability  
- No control over outcomes  

---

## Best Use Cases

- Student projects  
- Small experiments  
- Proof of concepts
