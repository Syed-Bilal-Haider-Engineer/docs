# ⚡ Agile Model

## Definition

Agile is an **iterative and incremental development model** focused on flexibility, collaboration, and customer feedback.

---

## Key Principles

- Continuous delivery  
- Customer collaboration  
- Responding to change  
- Working in short iterations (sprints)  

---

## Sprint Cycle

- 1–4 weeks per sprint  
- Planning → Development → Testing → Review  

---

## Characteristics

- Flexible and adaptive  
- Continuous feedback  
- Frequent releases  

---

## Advantages

- Very flexible  
- Fast delivery  
- High customer satisfaction  

---

## Disadvantages

- Less predictability  
- Requires experienced teams  
- Risk of scope creep  

---

## Best Use Cases

- Web applications  
- Mobile apps  
- Startups
