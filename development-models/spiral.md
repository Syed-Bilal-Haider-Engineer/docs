# 🌀 Spiral Model

## Definition

The Spiral Model combines **iterative development with risk analysis**, making it ideal for complex systems.

---

## Phases

1. Planning  
2. Risk Analysis  
3. Engineering  
4. Evaluation  

---

## Characteristics

- Risk-driven approach  
- Iterative cycles (spirals)  
- Focus on continuous risk evaluation  

---

## Advantages

- Strong risk management  
- Flexible and iterative  
- Suitable for large systems  

---

## Disadvantages

- Expensive  
- Complex to manage  
- Requires experienced team  

---

## Best Use Cases

- Aerospace systems  
- Banking systems  
- Large enterprise platforms
